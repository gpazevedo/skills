## Proposals not decided
- Move the fair onto the oval instead of the hall and courtyard: raised by Raj; status: no agreement recorded; Fiona noted the oval needs council sign-off under the shared-use agreement and there is no cover if it rains; Helen said it is "worth considering, but depends on council".
- Use the hall as a wet-weather backup if the fair moves to the oval: raised by Raj; status: no agreement recorded.
- Raise entry from $5 to $8 per family, with a free option for school card families: raised by Daniel; status: disputed by Siobhan (concern $8 might put school card families off) and by Raj (wants last year's actual numbers first; thinks the deficit was mostly ride hire, not the gate); discussion "left there, come back to it".
- Go cashless this year: raised by Siobhan; status: no agreement recorded; Fiona "fine with that in principle" but raised a concern about older grandparents.
- Hybrid payments, cash at the gate only and cards at stalls: raised by Helen ("maybe"); status: no agreement recorded.
- A second food truck: raised by Helen ("worth considering"); status: no agreement recorded.
- Book a jumping castle again from Bounce Bros (quoted ~$450 for the day last year): raised by Daniel ("Could do that again"); status: no agreement recorded; Raj noted the insurance condition that someone must be on it the whole time.
- A teacher-run stall (e.g. a repeat of Siobhan's class plant stall from 2025): raised by Fiona; status: depends on whether Siobhan's class wants to.
- A volunteer sign-up sheet on the school app instead of paper: raised by Daniel; status: "General nodding, no one said no", but nobody took it on (see open questions).
- Keep the float at $600, as last year: raised by Raj ("Probably the same"); status: no agreement recorded.
