# Tern Systems Pty Ltd: Support and Service-Level Policy

Version 2026.2, effective 1 August 2026.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 Business hours are 08:30 to 17:30 Australia/Perth time (AWST, UTC+8, no daylight saving), Monday to Friday, excluding Western Australian public holidays.

1.2 Ticket timestamps are in UTC. 08:30 to 17:30 AWST is 00:30 to 09:30 UTC on the same date.

1.3 The customer's support plan (Essentials, Business or Premier) is the one in the account record.

1.4 First response means the first contact with the customer by a support agent, by phone, email or portal. A ticket logged by an agent during a call with the customer counts as a first response when logged.

1.5 Resolution means that Tern has provided a fix or a workaround that restores the affected function and the ticket status has been set to Resolved. A ticket that reports more than one fault is resolved only when every fault on it is resolved.

## 2. Priorities

2.1 P1 (Critical): more than half of the customer's gateways are offline, or TernCloud is unavailable to all of the customer's users.

2.2 P2 (High): some of the customer's gateways are offline, or alarms, data delivery, reporting or user access fail for some gateways or users, and there is no practical workaround.

2.3 P3 (Normal): a fault as in 2.2 with a practical workaround, or any other impaired function.

2.4 P4 (Low): questions and requests.

2.5 The support agent sets the priority using these definitions. A customer may request a different priority; a priority applies only once recorded on the ticket.

## 3. Service-level targets

3.1 Targets by support plan and priority:

| Support plan | Priority | First response | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Premier | P1 | 1 hour, 24x7 | 12 hours, 24x7 |
| Premier | P2 | 2 business hours | 18 business hours |
| Premier | P3 | 4 business hours | 45 business hours |
| Business | P1 | 2 hours, 24x7 | 24 hours, 24x7 |
| Business | P2 | 4 business hours | 27 business hours |
| Business | P3 | 9 business hours | 72 business hours |
| Essentials | P1 | 4 business hours | 36 business hours |
| Essentials | P2 | 9 business hours | 54 business hours |
| Essentials | P3 | 18 business hours | 90 business hours |

3.2 P4 tickets: first response within 18 business hours on every plan; no resolution target.

3.3 Targets marked "24x7" run on calendar time. All others count business hours only.

## 4. The service-level clock

4.1 The clock starts when the ticket is created. Business-hours targets count only time inside business hours; for a ticket created outside them, counting starts when business hours next begin.

4.2 The clock is paused only while the ticket status is "Pending customer". It resumes when the status leaves "Pending customer". Paused time does not count toward any target.

4.3 If the priority changes, the resolution target becomes the target for the new priority, counted from ticket creation and excluding paused time.

4.4 A ticket is "breached" if its resolution target has passed unresolved, and "at risk" if less than 25% of its resolution target remains, measured on the same basis (calendar or business hours) as the target. Otherwise it is "within target".

## 5. Escalation

5.1 Tier 1 escalates to Tier 2 (Field Systems Engineering) when a ticket cannot be resolved with documented procedures, or when a P1 or P2 ticket is at risk.

5.2 When a P1 ticket, or an at-risk P2 ticket, is escalated, the Support Team Lead must be notified at escalation.

5.3 From escalation, Tier 2 owns the ticket, including customer updates and commitments already made.

## 6. Commitments, credits and refunds

6.1 Support agents may commit to the time of the next update and to actions they will take themselves.

6.2 Support agents must not promise a fix date, a service credit, a refund or compensation.

6.3 Only the Account Manager named in the account record, or the Head of Customer Support, may offer a service credit, refund or compensation. An offer is valid only when recorded in writing on the ticket or in the account record.

## 7. Customer updates

7.1 P1: an update at least every 4 hours, 24x7. P2: at least once each business day. P3 and P4: when there is news.
