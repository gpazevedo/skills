- Ticket pricing: Daniel proposed raising entry from $5 to $8 per family with a free option for school card families; not decided. Raj wants last year's actual numbers first, and the notes say "(left there, come back to it)".
- Payments: Siobhan proposed going cashless; Helen suggested a hybrid (cash at the gate only, cards at stalls); Fiona raised concerns about older grandparents. Not decided; pending card reader costs.
- Second food truck: Helen said it was "worth considering"; not decided.
- Jumping castle: Daniel mentioned Bounce Bros quoted ~$450 last year and "Could do that again"; not decided. If it goes ahead, someone must be on it the whole time (insurance condition), and no one is named for that.
- Teacher-run stall: Fiona suggested it; Siobhan is "happy to, if the class wants to", so it depends on her class's answer.
- Volunteers: roughly 25 volunteers are needed across the day. Daniel suggested a sign-up sheet on the school app; the notes record "General nodding, no one said no. Nobody took it on yet." Not recorded as a decision, and no owner or deadline.
- Float: Raj said last year's float was $600, "Probably the same"; not confirmed as decided.
- Deadline for Siobhan asking her class about the stall is not stated.
