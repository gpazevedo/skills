## Open questions and contradictions
- Venue: moving the fair onto the oval instead of the hall and courtyard, proposed by Raj, with the hall as a wet-weather backup. Not decided; it depends on council sign-off.
  Source: "Raj: we could move the whole thing onto the oval instead of the hall + courtyard." / "Helen: worth considering, but depends on council."
- Ticket price: Daniel proposed raising entry from $5 to $8 per family, with a free option for school card families. Siobhan raised concern about school card families; Raj wants last year's numbers before deciding. Left unresolved.
  Source: "Daniel: let's go with $8 then? with a free option for school card families" / "(left there, come back to it)"
- Payments: going cashless, proposed by Siobhan, or a hybrid (cash at the gate only, cards at stalls), suggested by Helen. Fiona is fine with cashless in principle but raised concern about older grandparents. No decision; card reader costs to be reported at the next meeting.
  Source: "Siobhan: what if we went cashless this year?" / "Helen: maybe a hybrid, cash at the gate only, cards at stalls?"
- Attractions: a second food truck, suggested by Helen.
  Source: "Helen: worth considering a second food truck, the queue for the sausage sizzle was 40 min."
- Attractions: hiring a jumping castle again (Bounce Bros quoted about $450 last year), raised by Daniel. If booked, someone must supervise it the whole time as an insurance condition (Raj); no one is assigned.
  Source: "Daniel mentioned Bounce Bros quoted ~$450 for a jumping castle for the day last year. Could do that again." / "Raj: if we do the castle we need someone on it the whole time, insurance condition."
- Attractions: a teacher-run stall, suggested by Fiona; depends on Siobhan's class wanting to.
  Source: "Fiona: would be good to have a teacher-run stall too"
- Decided or proposed? Volunteer sign-up sheet on the school app instead of paper, suggested by Daniel. The notes record no objection but no explicit agreement, and no owner. About 25 volunteers are needed (Helen's estimate).
  Source: "Daniel suggested a sign-up sheet on the school app instead of paper." / "General nodding, no one said no. Nobody took it on yet."
- Budget: keeping the float at $600, as last year, suggested by Raj. Not confirmed.
  Source: "Raj: float last year was $600. Probably the same."

## Handoff summary
The subcommittee agreed to hold the Autumn Fair on Saturday 20 March 2027, and Helen will put a save-the-date in the newsletter on Fri 13 Nov. Raj will circulate last year's income/expenses sheet by Mon 16 Nov and email council about using the oval by Fri 27 Nov. Siobhan will report card reader hire costs at the next meeting (Wed 9 Dec) and will ask her class about running a stall. Still unresolved are the venue (oval or hall and courtyard), the ticket price ($8 proposed, with a free option for school card families), cashless or hybrid payments, which attractions to book, the float amount, and who will run the volunteer sign-up sheet on the school app.
