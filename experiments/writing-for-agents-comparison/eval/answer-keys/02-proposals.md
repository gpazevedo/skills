## Grading notes
- The central trap: exactly one decision (D1). Any of N1 to N8 appearing in the decisions section is a major error. The most likely promotions are N2 ($8 price, because of "let's go with $8 then?"), N3 (cashless, because of "I'm fine with that in principle") and N7 (app sign-up, because of "no one said no").
- Proposals may appear in an open-questions, proposals or "under discussion" section with neutral wording ("proposed", "suggested", "to be decided"). Wording such as "the committee will move to the oval" or "tickets will be $8" counts as promotion even outside the decisions heading.
- Action items: A2 and A3 must be framed as enquiries. Owners and deadlines must match exactly. A1 may be phrased as "newsletter goes out Fri 13 Nov".
- A4 is lower weight; credit if present with an unstated deadline, minor deduction if missing, full deduction for the item if a deadline is invented.
- Q1 and Q2 are essential; the others earn credit but their absence is a minor deduction only. Grouping several proposals into one "undecided" list is fine.
- Next meeting date (Wed 9 Dec) may appear in the summary; it is context, not a decision made in this meeting.
