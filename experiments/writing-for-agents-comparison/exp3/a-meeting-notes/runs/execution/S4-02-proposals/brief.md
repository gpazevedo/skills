# Decision brief: Westbrook Primary School P&C: Autumn Fair subcommittee

Date: Wed 11 Nov 2026  |  Attendees: Helen Marsh (chair), Raj Patel (treasurer), Siobhan Kelly (Year 4 teacher, staff rep), Daniel Cho (parent rep), Fiona Brandt (principal); apologies: Kerry-Ann Moss

## Decisions
1. **The Autumn Fair will be held on Saturday 20 March 2027, not 13 March, which clashes with the district swimming carnival.**
   Evidence: "AGREED: Autumn Fair on Sat 20 Mar 2027." (Date section; preceded by "No objections to 20th.")

## Proposals not yet decided
- Move the fair from the hall and courtyard onto the oval, for more stall room and less congestion at the canteen end; depends on council sign-off under the shared-use agreement (raised by Raj)
- Use the hall as a wet-weather backup if the fair moves to the oval (raised by Raj)
- Raise entry from $5 to $8 per family, with a free option for school card families; Raj wants last year's actual numbers before anything is locked in (raised by Daniel)
- Go cashless this year (raised by Siobhan; Fiona "fine with that in principle" but concerned about older grandparents)
- Hybrid payments: cash at the gate only, cards at stalls (raised by Helen)
- Add a second food truck, as the sausage sizzle queue was 40 min (raised by Helen)
- Hire a jumping castle again from Bounce Bros, quoted about $450 for the day last year; insurance requires someone on it the whole time (raised by Daniel)
- Have a teacher-run stall, such as Siobhan's class plant stall from 2025 (raised by Fiona)
- Use a volunteer sign-up sheet on the school app instead of paper (raised by Daniel)
- Keep the float at $600, the same as last year (raised by Raj: "Probably the same.")
