# Quillbill Ltd: Customer Support Policy

Version 4.2, effective 1 July 2026. This policy applies to every Quillbill customer with an active support agreement.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 Business hours are 08:00 to 18:00 Europe/London time, Monday to Friday, excluding bank holidays in England. Between 25 October 2026 and 28 March 2027, Europe/London time is GMT, which is the same as UTC.

1.2 All ticket timestamps are recorded in UTC.

1.3 A customer's support level (Standard, Business or Enterprise) is the level recorded in the customer's account record.

1.4 First response means the first reply to the customer from a support agent, by email, portal or phone. Automated acknowledgements do not count.

1.5 Resolution means that Quillbill has provided a fix or a workaround that restores the affected function and the ticket status has been set to Resolved.

## 2. Priorities

2.1 P1 (Critical): Quillbill is unavailable to all of the customer's users, or invoice or payment data has been lost or corrupted.

2.2 P2 (High): a major function, such as invoice sending, payment collection or bank feeds, does not work and there is no practical workaround.

2.3 P3 (Normal): a function is impaired but a practical workaround exists.

2.4 P4 (Low): questions, how-to requests and feature requests.

2.5 Support sets the priority using these definitions. A customer may ask for a different priority; a change takes effect only when a support agent records it on the ticket.

## 3. Service-level targets

3.1 Targets by support level and priority:

| Support level | Priority | First response | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Enterprise | P1 | 30 minutes, 24x7 | 8 hours, 24x7 |
| Enterprise | P2 | 2 hours, 24x7 | 24 hours, 24x7 |
| Enterprise | P3 | 4 business hours | 30 business hours |
| Business | P1 | 1 hour, 24x7 | 12 hours, 24x7 |
| Business | P2 | 4 business hours | 20 business hours |
| Business | P3 | 8 business hours | 50 business hours |
| Standard | P1 | 4 business hours | 20 business hours |
| Standard | P2 | 8 business hours | 40 business hours |
| Standard | P3 | 16 business hours | 80 business hours |

3.2 For P4 tickets the first-response target is 16 business hours at every support level, and there is no resolution target.

3.3 Targets marked "24x7" run on calendar time. All other targets count business hours only.

## 4. The service-level clock

4.1 The clock starts when the ticket is created.

4.2 The clock is paused only while the ticket status is "Pending customer". It resumes when the status leaves "Pending customer".

4.3 Paused time does not count toward any target.

4.4 If the priority changes, the resolution target becomes the target for the new priority, counted from ticket creation and excluding paused time.

4.5 A ticket is "breached" if its resolution target has passed and it is not resolved, and "at risk" if less than 25% of its resolution target remains, measured on the same basis (calendar hours or business hours) as the target. Otherwise it is "within target".

## 5. Escalation

5.1 Tier 1 escalates a ticket to Tier 2 (Product Engineering Support) when it cannot be resolved with documented procedures, or when a P1 or P2 ticket is at risk.

5.2 From escalation, Tier 2 owns the ticket, including customer updates and any commitments already made to the customer.

5.3 The Support Duty Manager must be told of every P1 ticket and of every P2 ticket that is breached.

## 6. Commitments, credits and refunds

6.1 Support agents may commit to the time of the next update and to actions they will take themselves.

6.2 Support agents must not promise a fix date for a product defect, a release date, a service credit or a refund.

6.3 Only the Customer Success Manager named in the customer's account record, or the Head of Support, may offer a service credit or refund. An offer is valid only when it is recorded in writing on the ticket or in the account record.

## 7. Customer updates

7.1 P1 tickets: an update at least every 2 hours. P2 tickets: an update at least once each business day. P3 and P4 tickets: an update when there is news.
