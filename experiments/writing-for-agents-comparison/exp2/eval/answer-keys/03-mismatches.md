## Grading notes
- Six clean items: 1, 2, 3, 5 (or 8), 6, 9.
- Traps: amount mismatch (7), missing receipt file (10), the same receipt submitted twice under two references with a wrong date on the second line (5 and 8), receipt dated outside the trip (11), ticket in another person's name paid on the claimant's card (4).
- For items 4, 8 and 11, reject and needs information both earn verdict credit; what matters is that they are not paid and the specific evidence (name, matching check number, date) is cited. Generic grounds such as "receipt problem" earn partial credit only.
- For item 7, the expected amount is 23.40 under 3.2. A review that holds the whole item as needs information loses partial credit because the policy already settles it.
- For item 1, a reviewer must not flag the purchase date (29 September) as outside the trip; 4.1 allows advance booking of travel.
