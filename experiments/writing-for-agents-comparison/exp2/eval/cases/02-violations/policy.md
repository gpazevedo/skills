# Harrowfield Engineering Pty Ltd: Business Expense Policy

Document HR-FIN-07, version 3, effective 1 July 2026. Owner: Finance.

## 1. Scope and currency
1.1 This policy applies to employees claiming reimbursement of costs they have paid on company business. All amounts are in Australian dollars (AUD) and include GST.
1.2 Claims must be lodged within 60 days of the date of the expense.

## 2. Approval
2.1 Every claim is approved by the employee's line manager. A claim totalling more than AUD 2,000.00 also requires approval by the General Manager.

## 3. Receipts
3.1 Every claimed item must be supported by a receipt.
3.2 For any item of AUD 82.50 or more, the receipt must be a tax invoice or itemised receipt showing what was purchased. A card terminal slip or a bank statement on its own is not sufficient.
3.3 An item that does not meet clause 3.1 or 3.2 is not paid until the required receipt is supplied.

## 4. Air travel
4.1 Domestic flights are economy class. A General Manager may approve business class in writing for a single flight with a scheduled duration over 5 hours; the written approval must be attached to the claim.
4.2 A fare in a higher class than clause 4.1 allows is not reimbursed, in whole or in part.

## 5. Accommodation
5.1 The nightly cap for accommodation (room rate) is: Sydney and Melbourne AUD 280.00; other capital cities AUD 230.00; elsewhere AUD 180.00.
5.2 The amount up to the cap is reimbursed. The amount above the cap is not reimbursed.

## 6. Meals while travelling
6.1 When travelling overnight, the employee's own meals are reimbursed at actual cost up to the following amount per meal: breakfast AUD 30.00; lunch AUD 40.00; dinner AUD 70.00.
6.2 Alcohol is not reimbursed, except as part of client entertainment under clause 8.

## 7. Ground transport
7.1 Taxis, rideshare and public transport between airports, hotels and work locations are reimbursed.
7.2 Airport parking is reimbursed up to AUD 30.00 per calendar day or part day.

## 8. Client entertainment
8.1 Meals with clients are reimbursed up to AUD 120.00 per head, including alcohol, counting every attendee (employees included).
8.2 The claim must list every attendee's name and organisation and state the business purpose. An entertainment item without this list is not paid until the list is supplied.

## 9. Other business costs
9.1 Printing, stationery and materials bought for company work are reimbursed at cost.

## 10. Not reimbursable
10.1 The following are not reimbursed: personal items; gym or fitness facility fees; fines and penalties; travel insurance (employees are covered by the company's corporate travel policy); laundry on trips of fewer than 5 nights; minibar and in-room entertainment.
