- Claim form arithmetic: the 11 items sum to NZD 1,833.40, matching the stated claimed total.
- Duplicate claim (item 11): same claimant, same invoice number INV-88213, same date and amount as the paid September claim. Clause 7.1 ("reimbursed once per membership year") points the same way. Item 11 is also dated 8 September 2026, outside the 19 to 22 October trip the claim describes, which the claim form does not explain.
- Unmatched receipt R12: pharmacy purchase of sunscreen and cold and flu capsules, NZD 31.40. It matches no claimed item and has not been added to the claim. Finance does not process receipts not listed on the claim form (clause 2.2); if it were claimed, clause 9.1 lists "Personal items, including toiletries and medicines" as not reimbursable.
- Claimed pre-approval: the claimant's email of 27 October says "Hemi pre-approved all of this before I went, including the hotel". This is the claimant's statement, not written pre-approval from the director as clause 3.1 defines it, and nothing from the director is attached. Only item 9 needs pre-approval under 3.1; no item is client entertainment under 6.3.
- Fast-track request: the email asks for payment in this week's run. Clause 3.3 says approved claims are paid in the fortnightly payment run; the policy names no fast-track process, so this is for the approver or Finance to decide.
- Item 5 reasonableness: clause 6.2 does not define "reasonable" or set a per-head limit for working meals with colleagues. The meal works out at NZD 68.40 per head; the 6.1 dinner cap (NZD 60.00) applies to staff's own meals and the 6.3 cap (NZD 90.00 per head) to client entertainment, so neither applies directly. All five attendees are Marrow & Pike staff and the receipt shows no alcohol. The claimant did not claim a separate dinner on 20 October.
- Flights are not claimed, consistent with clause 4.1 (booked through the travel agent).
- Submission timing: all items, including the 8 September membership, are within the 90-day limit of clause 1.2 (claim submitted 27 October 2026).
- Receipts R01 and R06 are not headed as itemised tax invoices; both are under NZD 50.00, so clause 2.1 requires only a receipt.
