# Marrow & Pike Architects Ltd: Expense Reimbursement Policy

Revision 5, March 2026. Applies to all staff.

## 1. General
1.1 The company reimburses, in New Zealand dollars (NZD), costs that staff pay personally on company business. Amounts include GST.
1.2 Claims must be submitted within 90 days of the expense.
1.3 A cost that has been reimbursed on an earlier claim cannot be claimed again.
1.4 Each claim is approved by the claimant's director.

## 2. Receipts
2.1 Every claimed item requires a receipt. Items above NZD 50.00 require an itemised tax invoice.
2.2 Each receipt must be referenced against a line on the claim form. Finance does not process receipts that are not listed on the claim form.

## 3. Pre-approval
3.1 Any single claimed item above NZD 500.00 requires written pre-approval from the claimant's director, attached to the claim. Written pre-approval means an email sent by the director, or a pre-approval form signed by the director, dated before the expense was incurred.
3.2 An item that requires pre-approval is held, and not paid, until the written pre-approval is attached.
3.3 Approved claims are paid in the fortnightly payment run.

## 4. Travel
4.1 Domestic flights are booked through the company's travel agent and billed to the company; they are not claimed by staff.
4.2 Taxis and rideshare are reimbursed for travel to and from airports and for site visits. Parking at client sites is reimbursed.

## 5. Accommodation
5.1 The nightly cap is NZD 240.00 in Auckland and Wellington and NZD 190.00 elsewhere. The amount above the cap is not reimbursed.

## 6. Meals
6.1 When travelling overnight, staff are reimbursed for their own meals up to: breakfast NZD 25.00; lunch NZD 35.00; dinner NZD 60.00.
6.2 Reasonable costs of working meals with colleagues during a business trip are reimbursed. The claim must list the attendees.
6.3 Client entertainment requires the director's pre-approval regardless of amount and is capped at NZD 90.00 per head.
6.4 Alcohol is reimbursed only as part of client entertainment under clause 6.3.

## 7. Professional development and memberships
7.1 Membership of a professional body that is required for the staff member's role is reimbursed once per membership year.
7.2 Continuing professional development courses are reimbursed with the director's approval.

## 8. Project costs
8.1 Printing and materials bought for project work are reimbursed at cost.

## 9. Not reimbursable
9.1 Personal items, including toiletries and medicines; fines; minibar and in-room entertainment; travel insurance.
