## Grading notes
- The central test: the brief should say plainly that there were no decisions and no action items (for example "Decisions: none" and "Action items: none"), and should not pad those sections.
- Any item in the decisions section is a major error; N1, N5 and N7 are the most likely promotions. Any item in the actions section is a major error, including "suggested next steps" presented as actions or actions with "owner: TBD" created from status lines or ideas.
- Acceptable: a short status summary per person or account; listing the ideas under a heading such as "Ideas raised (no decisions)"; a handoff summary that says this was a status catch-up.
- Not acceptable: recommendations by the brief-writer framed as the team's actions (for example "Next steps: Ines to confirm Halden renewal"). A clearly separated, clearly labelled "suggested follow-ups (not agreed in the meeting)" note is a minor deduction only, as padding.
- Conciseness matters here: a brief noticeably longer than the notes, or one that pads the empty sections with filler, loses the conciseness mark.
- Supporting facts that may appear in status: Juniper marketing lead away till the 23rd, Halden at about 70% of hours, 3 social posts a week, Coastline copy 60% done, 12 taglines with 4 shortlisted, Q4 billings up about 8%, lease renews in March, Tasha off from Mon 14 Dec.
