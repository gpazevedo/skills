# Tessaly Foundation: Staff Travel and Expenses Policy

Version 4.2, effective 1 April 2026. Owner: Finance Director.

## 1. Scope
1.1 This policy covers expenses incurred by Foundation staff on approved Foundation business. Reimbursement is paid in pounds sterling (GBP) to the staff member's UK bank account.
1.2 Only the staff member who incurred an expense for themselves may claim it. Costs incurred for another person, including a colleague, are claimed by that person on their own claim.

## 2. Claims
2.1 Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip.
2.2 Each expense may be claimed once only.
2.3 A claim above GBP 1,000.00 requires approval by the Finance Director in addition to the line manager.

## 3. Receipts
3.1 Each claimed item must be supported by a receipt or invoice showing the merchant, date, items and amount. Per diem allowances under clause 7.2 need no receipt.
3.2 Where the amount claimed differs from the amount on the receipt, the lower of the two is reimbursed.
3.3 If a receipt is lost, the staff member must submit a missing-receipt declaration countersigned by their line manager. An item with neither a receipt nor a declaration is not paid until one is provided.
3.4 Travel tickets and accommodation bookings that carry a passenger or guest name must be in the claimant's name.

## 4. Trip dates
4.1 Expenses must be incurred between the approved start and end dates of the trip. Travel, accommodation and event registration may be booked and paid before the trip starts.

## 5. Travel
5.1 Rail travel is standard class.
5.2 Flights are economy class where the scheduled flight time is under 6 hours.
5.3 Taxis may be used between stations, airports and hotels, or where public transport is not available.
5.4 Local public transport is reimbursed at cost.

## 6. Accommodation
6.1 Nightly caps (room rate including taxes): London GBP 180.00; elsewhere in the UK GBP 120.00; Germany EUR 160.00. The amount above the cap is not reimbursed.

## 7. Meals and subsistence
7.1 UK trips: actual meal costs are reimbursed against receipts up to the following amounts per day: breakfast GBP 12.00 (where not included in the room rate); lunch GBP 15.00; evening meal GBP 30.00. The amount above these limits is not reimbursed.
7.2 Overseas trips: a daily per diem is paid instead of meal costs. Meal receipts are not reimbursed for any day on which per diem is paid. Germany: full day EUR 56.00. The day of departure from the UK and the day of return to the UK are partial days, paid at EUR 42.00 each.
7.3 Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## 8. Foreign currency
8.1 Amounts paid in a foreign currency are converted to GBP at the Foundation's fixed rate for the calendar month in which the expense was incurred, not at the rate on a card or bank statement. A per diem is converted at the rate for the month of the days it covers.
8.2 Fixed rates for 2026: September, EUR 1.00 = GBP 0.86; October, EUR 1.00 = GBP 0.85; November, EUR 1.00 = GBP 0.84.
8.3 Converted amounts are rounded to the nearest penny for each claimed item.
8.4 Bank or card foreign transaction fees are not reimbursed.

## 9. Other expenses
9.1 Event registration fees, and printing and materials for Foundation work, are reimbursed at cost.
9.2 The following are not reimbursed: personal items; travel insurance (the Foundation holds a group policy); fines; upgrades to a higher class of travel.
